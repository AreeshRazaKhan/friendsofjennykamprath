## 2. What we will change, in plain language

### 2.1 Make the website colors match your campaign signs

**Right now.** The website uses a dark navy blue paired with a bold red.

**What we'll do.** Once we have your actual sign artwork (or the exact colors used by your sign printer), we'll update the entire website to match. Because colors are handled in one central place, the update rolls across every page at once — buttons, headings, accents, everything.

**What we need from you.** A clear photo of your campaign sign in good daylight, or a file from the sign printer with the exact colors.

---

### 2.2 Make your name and position much larger everywhere

**Right now.** On the homepage, "For Washington County Chair" shows up in very small uppercase letters near the top, and your name "Jenny Kamprath" does not appear at the top of the page at all — only further down in the paragraphs. The About Jenny page has the same issue.

**What we'll do.** We will add a prominent block at the top of the homepage and the About Jenny page that displays:

- **JENNY KAMPRATH** — large, in the campaign style
- **For Washington County Chair** — directly underneath, clearly readable

So the first thing anyone sees is your name and the position you're running for.

**What we need from you.** Do you want your name styled the same way as your logo (with "JENNY" in red and "KAMPRATH" in white), or shown simply as "Jenny Kamprath" all in one color?

---

### 2.3 Tighten the writing and remove repetition

**Right now.** A few messages repeat across the site — especially "not a career politician / former business owner" and "not tied to insiders." They appear in multiple sections on the homepage and again on the About Jenny page.

**What we'll do.** We will tighten the copy so each section says something different. Our recommendation is:

- **Homepage:** trim one of the repeated action sections (the current "What Jenny Will Do on Day One" list overlaps with the Priorities cards just above it). We'll either remove it or rewrite it as a distinct "First 30 Days" promise.
- **About Jenny page:** collapse the three image-and-text blocks in the middle from three into two, because the first two currently say similar things.
- **Keep all your current pages.** We don't think you need to lose any pages — tightening the words does the job.

**What we need from you.** Are you comfortable keeping all your current pages (Home, Meet Jenny, Events, Ask Jenny, Contact, Volunteer) as long as the writing is tightened? Or would you prefer to actually merge two pages together?

---

### 2.4 Add a new Community Endorsements page (self-updating)

**What you asked for.** A dedicated page listing community members who endorse your campaign, with the ability to add new names yourself as they come in — without needing a developer.

**What we'll do.** We'll build the page so it pulls its list directly from your campaign management system (GoHighLevel). Adding a new endorser will look like this:

1. You log into GoHighLevel.
2. You click "Add new endorsement."
3. You type in the person's name, their title (e.g. "Small Business Owner, Hillsboro"), and optionally a quote and photo.
4. You hit save. Within about a minute, their endorsement appears on the website automatically.

The new page will live in the main menu between "Meet Jenny" and "Events." The homepage will also show the top 3–4 endorsers with a "See all endorsements →" link.

**What we need from you.**

- Do you want photos of endorsers, or just their names and titles?
- A short starter list of 3–5 endorsers so the page isn't empty on launch (we can help you enter them).

She is not a career politician — she is a working Oregonian who decided to stop complaining and start running."*

We'll update this in all three places it currently appears.

**What we need from you.**
