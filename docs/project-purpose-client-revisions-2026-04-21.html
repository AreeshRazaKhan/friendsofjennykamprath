2. What we will change, in plain language

2.1 Make the website colors match your campaign signs

Right now. The website uses a dark navy blue paired with a bold red.

What we'll do. Once we have your actual sign artwork (or the exact colors used by your sign printer), we'll update the entire website to match. Because colors are handled in one central place, the update rolls across every page at once — buttons, headings, accents, everything.

What we need from you. A clear photo of your campaign sign in good daylight, or a file from the sign printer with the exact colors.

2.2 Make your name and position much larger everywhere

Right now. On the homepage, "For Washington County Chair" shows up in very small uppercase letters near the top, and your name "Jenny Kamprath" does not appear at the top of the page at all — only further down in the paragraphs. The About Jenny page has the same issue.

What we'll do. We will add a prominent block at the top of the homepage and the About Jenny page that displays:

  • JENNY KAMPRATH — large, in the campaign style
  • For Washington County Chair — directly underneath, clearly readable

So the first thing anyone sees is your name and the position you're running for.

What we need from you. Do you want your name styled the same way as your logo (with "JENNY" in red and "KAMPRATH" in white), or shown simply as "Jenny Kamprath" all in one color?

2.3 Tighten the writing and remove repetition

Right now. A few messages repeat across the site — especially "not a career politician / former business owner" and "not tied to insiders." They appear in multiple sections on the homepage and again on the About Jenny page.

What we'll do. We will tighten the copy so each section says something different. Our recommendation is:

  • Homepage: trim one of the repeated action sections (the current "What Jenny Will Do on Day One" list overlaps with the Priorities cards just above it). We'll either remove it or rewrite it as a distinct "First 30 Days" promise.
  • About Jenny page: collapse the three image-and-text blocks in the middle from three into two, because the first two currently say similar things.
  • Keep all your current pages. We don't think you need to lose any pages — tightening the words does the job.

What we need from you. Are you comfortable keeping all your current pages (Home, Meet Jenny, Events, Ask Jenny, Contact, Volunteer) as long as the writing is tightened? Or would you prefer to actually merge two pages together?

2.4 Add a new Community Endorsements page (self-updating)

What you asked for. A dedicated page listing community members who endorse your campaign, with the ability to add new names yourself as they come in — without needing a developer.

What we'll do. We'll build the page so it pulls its list directly from your campaign management system (GoHighLevel). Adding a new endorser will look like this:

  1. You log into GoHighLevel.
  2. You click "Add new endorsement."
  3. You type in the person's name, their title (e.g. "Small Business Owner, Hillsboro"), and optionally a quote and photo.
  4. You hit save. Within about a minute, their endorsement appears on the website automatically.

The new page will live in the main menu between "Meet Jenny" and "Events." The homepage will also show the top 3–4 endorsers with a "See all endorsements →" link.

What we need from you.

  • Do you want photos of endorsers, or just their names and titles?
  • A short starter list of 3–5 endorsers so the page isn't empty on launch (we can help you enter them).

2.5 Add "affordability" to the red-tape message

We will update the wording everywhere that phrase appears on the site:

Before After
Cut the red tape strangling building and business Cut the red tape strangling building, business, and affordability
Cut Red Tape Strangling Building & Business Cut Red Tape Strangling Building, Business & Affordability
Cut red tape that blocks housing and burdens small business Cut red tape that blocks housing, affordability, and small business

We'll also add a line under that priority specifically about affordability, so it isn't just a word drop-in.

2.6 Reword the "former small business owner" line

Right now. The homepage and the About Jenny page describe you as "a former small business owner and entrepreneur." You're right that this makes it sound past-tense.

What we'll do. Replace those lines with wording that shows you are working with real businesses every day. A draft for you to approve:

"Jenny Kamprath is in the trenches every day — working in sales, talking with Oregon business owners big and small, and hearing first-hand how policies out of Salem and the county are making it harder to stay in business. She is not a career politician — she is a working Oregonian who decided to stop complaining and start running."

We'll update this in all three places it currently appears.

What we need from you.

  • Is the draft above the right tone?
  • Do you want us to keep any reference to past small-business ownership (as credibility context), or drop it completely in favor of the current-day framing?

2.7 Add the "entrenched insiders" message

We will add your line to the site:

"If we keep electing the same insiders and entrenched approaches, we can't expect different results. It's time to bring a new perspective and accountability to county leadership. Washington County deserves a leader who will listen and act, and deliver real change."

Where we'd put it.

  • Primary placement: Replace the paragraph in the "Powered by the People" section on the homepage with this quote. That section already has the visual weight to carry it.
  • Secondary placement: Pull out the last sentence — "Washington County deserves a leader who will listen and act, and deliver real change" — as a large quote on the About Jenny page.

2.8 More variety in the photos

Right now. A lot of the website's photos come from the same event, which you correctly noticed.

What we'll do. Swap in the new photos you've sent, plus the two from the old website — the Hillsboro Hops baseball photo and the door-knocking group photo. We'll spread them across:

  • The "Powered by the People" section on the homepage
  • The Endorsements / community section
  • The three image blocks on the About Jenny page

What we need from you.

  • Please send the highest-resolution versions of every photo (directly from your phone or camera, not compressed).
  • For each photo, a one-line description of who/where/what is happening (e.g. "Jenny door-knocking with volunteers in Beaverton, March 2026"). We need this so the website is accessible for people using screen readers.

4. The order we'll work in

To keep things easy to review, we'll release the updates in small batches:

  1. First release — Wording changes. Adding "affordability," rewording the "former small business owner" line, and inserting the "entrenched insiders" message. Fastest and lowest risk.
  2. Second release — Larger name and position. Making your name and the position you're running for much more prominent on the homepage and About Jenny page.
  3. Third release — New Endorsements page. Building the page and connecting it to GoHighLevel so you can add names yourself.
  4. Fourth release — New photos. Swapping in the Hops photo, door-knocking photo, and the other new images you've sent.
  5. Fifth release — Color update. Matching the website colors to your campaign signs (we need the sign artwork first).
  6. Sixth release — Page tightening (if you approve). Trimming the repetitive sections we pointed out in section 2.3.
